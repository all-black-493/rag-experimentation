# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility and Schedule

Full-time employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Remote workdays must be
scheduled with your manager at least 24 hours in advance.

## Workspace

Employees are expected to maintain a dedicated, ergonomic workspace for remote work. A
one-time home office stipend of $500 is available to cover setup costs such as a desk,
chair, or monitor arm.

## Availability

All employees, remote or in-office, must be reachable during core collaboration hours of
10am to 3pm in their local time zone.

## Security

Remote access to internal systems requires an active VPN connection. Working from a
country other than your country of employment requires HR approval submitted at least
2 weeks in advance.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and monitor for remote work. Additional equipment, such as
a keyboard or webcam, may be reimbursed up to $200 per year.
