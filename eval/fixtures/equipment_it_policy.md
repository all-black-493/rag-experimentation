# Equipment and IT Policy

## Provisioning

New employees receive a laptop within 3 business days of their start date. Laptops are
refreshed on a 3-year cycle.

## Software

Installing software on company devices requires IT approval, unless the software is on
the pre-approved list.

## Loss and Theft

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Personal Use

Limited personal use of company equipment is permitted, provided it does not interfere
with work duties.

## Bring Your Own Device

Employees who wish to access company systems from a personal device must enroll that
device in mobile device management (MDM).

## Support

IT support is available from 8am to 6pm on weekdays. Emergency support is available
24/7 through the on-call line.
