# Data Handling Policy

## Classification

Customer data is classified into three tiers: Public, Internal, and Restricted.

## Protection

Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit using AES-256. Access to
Restricted data requires approval from both a manager and the security team.

## Retention and Deletion

Customer records are retained for 7 years after account closure. Data deletion requests
must be fulfilled within 30 days of receipt.

## Third Parties

Any third-party data processor must sign a data processing agreement before being
integrated with company systems.

## Breach Notification

Data breaches involving customer personal information must be reported to affected
customers within 72 hours of confirmation.
