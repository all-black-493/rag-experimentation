# Travel and Expense Policy

## Lodging

Employees may expense up to $150 per night for hotel stays within the continental United States.
International hotel stays are capped at $220 per night. Any stay above these caps requires
pre-approval from a director or above.

## Airfare

Economy class flights are standard for all trips. Business class requires VP approval for
flights with a scheduled duration over 6 hours.

## Meals

The per diem for meals is $65 per day for domestic travel and $85 per day for international
travel. Per diem amounts are not reduced for partial travel days.

## Ground Transportation

Personal vehicle use for business travel is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Rideshare and taxi
fares are reimbursable with a receipt.

## Receipts and Submission

Receipts are required for any single expense over $25. All travel expenses must be submitted
for reimbursement within 30 days of the trip's end date. Expenses submitted after 30 days
will not be reimbursed except with VP approval.

## Booking

Domestic trips must be booked through the approved travel portal at least 7 days in advance.
International trips must be booked at least 14 days in advance. Exceptions require manager
approval and a documented business justification.

## Currency

For international expenses, the reimbursement amount uses the currency conversion rate in
effect on the date of purchase, not the date of submission.
