# Leave Policy

## Vacation

Employees receive 15 days of paid vacation per year for their first 3 years of employment,
increasing to 20 days per year after 3 years. Up to 5 unused vacation days may roll over
into the next calendar year; any remaining balance beyond that is forfeited.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Unused sick days do not roll over
to the following year.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive
6 weeks of paid parental leave.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees receive 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family
member, and 2 days for the death of an extended family member.

## Jury Duty

Jury duty leave is fully paid with no cap on the number of days.

## Requesting Leave

Leave requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance, except for sick leave, which
may be requested with no advance notice.
