# Security Incident Response Policy

## Reporting

All suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of
detection. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 2 hours.

## Triage

The on-call security engineer is responsible for triaging incidents within 30 minutes of
the report.

## Escalation

Critical incidents, including data breaches and active exploitation, require executive
notification within 4 hours of confirmation.

## Post-Incident Review

Post-incident reviews must be completed within 5 business days of resolution.

## Access Controls

Multi-factor authentication is required for all production system access. Passwords for
privileged accounts must be rotated every 90 days.

## Training

All employees must complete security awareness training annually.
